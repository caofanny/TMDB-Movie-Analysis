D3 Breakeven Profit/Loss

Scatterplot with Break-even, Profit, and Loss Analysis - Interactive Dropdown

This scatterplot shows whether each movie had a loss, profit, or broke even with their initial investment. While there is an general positive trend between budget and revenue, higher revenue does not necessarily translate to a higher profit. Lower budget movies of less than $50 million accounts for majority of the highly profitable movies, earning more than 5 times their budget. As the budget increases from 50 million to 100 million, the distribution of highly profitable movies becomes more dispersed, while the profitable movies, earning to to five times their budget, becomes more concentrated. This pattern suggests that although higher budgets often secure greater revenues, they may face difficulty in achieving high profit ratios. The sparse presence of movies with losses suggest that most movies tend to at least break even or generate a profit.
