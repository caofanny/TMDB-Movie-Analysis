Scatterplot of Cast Size and Revenue

This is a scatterplot that displays the relationship between a movie's cast size and revenue in U.S. dollars.
Most of the data points appear to cluster in the bottom right of the graph but there are also a lot of points scattered randomly across the graph. It's important that there is an outlier point that showcases a large cast size and converted revenue, however, since it’s just one point we can’t use this to support an argument supporting an existing relationship. This leads us to believe that there appears to be no correlation between the cast size of a movie and the amount of revenue that was produced.Therefore, when planning for the production of a new film, cast size doesn’t appear to be a useful variable to know and control to maximize revenue.
